### UET CE Department - History, Accreditation and Programs (Fall 2026)

UET Lahore states it was the first institute in Pakistan to offer undergraduate computing education. It started a four-year B.Sc. (Hons) in Computer Science and Engineering in 2001; for students enrolling from September 2003 onward this was renamed and modified into the four-year B.Sc. Computer Engineering (CE) programme. The CE programme has been accredited by the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC) since it began, and the department became a separate entity in 2020.

Programmes offered: Undergraduate - B.Sc. Computer Engineering, B.Sc. Cyber Security, B.Sc. Information Systems and Technology. Postgraduate - M.Sc. Computer Engineering, Ph.D. Computer Engineering.

Laboratories: Circuits and Electronics Systems; AI and Embedded Systems; Computer Systems; Project; Postgraduate Research; Industrial Automation.

The department says its graduates work at international firms including Microsoft Bing, Google, Amazon, Oracle, IBM and Salesforce, and in local industry at companies such as Techlogix, Powersoft19, NetSol, i2c, Xavor and Mentor Graphics.

### UET CE - Contact-Hour and Credit-Hour Rules (Subject Types A, B, C)

A contact hour is a 50-minute period with students. A credit hour is linked to one or more contact hours per week, depending on subject type and semester.

- Type-A (Theory): lectures plus home assignments. Contact hours per credit hour per week: Fall/Spring 1, Summer 2. Assessment: mid-term (at least 1 hr) 30%; comprehensive final (at least 1.5 hrs, 20% pre-mid content) 40%; quizzes/assignments etc. 30%.
- Type-B (Practical): experiments, studio, design, drawing, projects. Contact hours per credit hour per week: Fall/Spring 2-3, Summer 4-6. Each experiment or task is a separate assessment instrument; combined performance decides the grade.
- Type-C (Comprehensive project): supervised design and development over 1-2 semesters. Contact hours per credit hour per week: Fall/Spring 2-4. Continuous assessment 60%; terminal viva-voce/jury 40%; external examiners required.

### UET CE - Grading System (Letter Grades and Relative Grading)

Theory (Type-A) subjects are graded on a relative scale after consulting the department Chairperson; Type-B and Type-C subjects may be graded on an absolute scale if the teacher considers it appropriate.

| Grade | A+ | A | A- | B+ | B | B- | C+ | C | C- | D+ | D | F | W | WF | I | IP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grade points | 4.0 | 4.0 | 3.7 | 3.3 | 3.0 | 2.7 | 2.3 | 2.0 | 1.7 | 1.3 | 1.0 | 0 | - | - | - | - |

Relative grading method: the teacher sets a minimum mastery threshold for passing (below it: F) and an expected maximum threshold (above it: A+, meant only for exceptional performance). Students between the thresholds are ranked and the mean and standard deviation are calculated; the cluster within half a standard deviation of the mean may be graded C+ or B-. Other grades are assigned by clusters (classes under 100) or a normal curve (classes over 100). A class need not receive every grade, and an upper limit on the share of students receiving a particular grade may be applied.

### UET CE - GPA, Special Grades, Probation and Degree Rules

GPA = sum(GPi x CHi) / sum(CHi) over the semester's subjects; CGPA uses the same formula over all subjects to date. The Controller of Examinations computes and notifies results at the end of each semester.

- W (withdrawal): student-initiated, by week 6 (Fall/Spring) or week 3 (Summer); excluded from GPA.
- WF (forced withdrawal): for attendance shortage or disciplinary action; excluded from GPA. A student who neither drops nor attempts any assessment receives F.
- I (incomplete): interim grade for illness or other acceptable reasons; must be completed in the following regular semester or it becomes F. IP (in progress): for Type-C subjects spanning more than one semester.
- Repetition: allowed within registration limits; the new grade replaces the old one, higher or lower, and repeated subjects carry an R suffix.
- Cheating: the teacher may award F after giving the student a chance to defend himself.
- Probation: CGPA below 2.0 after any semester. Separation: first-year students after 2 consecutive probation semesters; second-year after 3. No academic separation after the second year.
- Degree award: minimum CGPA 2.0, all required credit hours completed, no outstanding compulsory subject with W/WF/I/F.
- Degree with Honours: CGPA 3.70 or above, with no repeated subjects, no W/WF and no F. Medals and merit positions go only to Honours graduates.
- Dean's Honour Roll: semester GPA 3.7 or above with at least 15 CH and no W/WF/F (not awarded in Summer).
- Change of discipline: allowed only after first year, if the student's admission merit meets the target programme's minimum and a seat is available.
- Grievances: during the semester, petition to the Chairperson, who forms a 3-member committee that decides within 5 days. After results, Grade Change Request (Form 3) within one week, with changes approved by the Dean.

### UET ECAT 2026 - Test Structure and Merit Computation

ECAT 2026 test structure: 100 minutes; 10 English comprehension questions plus 30 questions in each of three subjects. The ECAT 2026 tests were computer-based. Relevant combinations: English, Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry; or English, Physics, Mathematics, Computer Science. Marking: 4 marks per correct answer, 0 for no answer, maximum 400. UET announced no negative marking for 2026, and there is no minimum passing threshold. ECAT Fall 2026 (Second) was held 16-21 June 2026.

Merit computation: ECAT weight is 33% for all streams. Academic weight: Intermediate (F.Sc./ICS) - First-year (Part-I) percentage 50% + Matric 17%; O/A Level - Class 11 (O-Level equivalent) 67% with IBCC conversion; DAE - sum of first- and second-year percentages 67%; B.Sc. Engineering Technology - 7-semester CGPA 67% (2.5 = 60%, 4.0 = 90%, interpolated).

- The minimum aggregate for any undergraduate admission is 50%. Ties are judged to four decimal places after truncation, and all candidates tied for the last seat are admitted.
- Hafiz-e-Quran candidates can receive 0-20 extra marks after an oral test (17 Jul 2026). Sportspersons can receive up to 10 marks added to Intermediate marks after a sports test (20 Jul 2026).
- Merit is calculated separately within each seat category. Higher-preference transfers happen automatically up to the 4th merit list, and unused reserved seats go to Punjab open merit.
- Example: an applicant with 85% ECAT, 90% F.Sc. Part-I and 92% Matric has an aggregate of 0.33 x 85 + 0.50 x 90 + 0.17 x 92 = 88.69% (illustrative calculation).
