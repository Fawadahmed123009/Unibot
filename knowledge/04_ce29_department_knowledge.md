### Q: How are course marks divided between midterm, final examination and sessional? (CE-29 / Session 2024)

This section records the examination marks distribution given in the examination rules row for batch CE-29.

- Answer: 30% midterm, 40% final examination, 30% sessional (quizzes, assignments, attendance and class participation). The midterm is the mid-semester examination and the final is the end-semester examination. The sheet says to confirm whether this distribution applies to every course before treating it as a university-wide rule. This information is not yet verified against an official source; please confirm with the department office.
- Category: Examination rules
- Source: Department Knowledge Collection Sheet, Session 2024 - CE-29, page 1 (priority 1)
