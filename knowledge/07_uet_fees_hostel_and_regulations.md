### UET Fall 2026 - Fee Categories for Undergraduate Engineering

UET's official Fall 2026 fee breakup has two categories: subsidized and partially subsidized. The same university fee schedule applies across undergraduate engineering programs, so Computer Engineering students should confirm their own category in the admission offer or on the student portal.

> Source note: Whether a student is subsidized or partially subsidized changes the total program cost by roughly Rs. 350,000, so the category should be confirmed on the offer letter.

### UET Fall 2026 - Total Program Fee (8 and 10 Semester)

For the standard 8-semester program the official semester totals are Rs. 674,114 (subsidized) and Rs. 1,024,040 (partially subsidized). For the 10-semester variant the totals are Rs. 871,162 and Rs. 1,326,222 respectively. First-semester totals are Rs. 107,871 (subsidized) and Rs. 155,177 (partially subsidized); later semesters vary mainly because of the final-semester degree/DMC charge.

### UET Fall and Spring - Hostel Charges

For Fall and Spring semesters, subsidized hostel accommodation is Rs. 11,644 for a dormitory or Rs. 15,846 for a cubical per semester. Non-subsidized rates are Rs. 14,512 (dormitory) and Rs. 21,582 (cubical) per semester.

### UET - Semester System, Credit Hour Limits and Academic Year

UET operates a semester system with Fall and Spring as regular semesters plus an optional summer semester. First- and second-year students may register for up to 19 credit hours in Fall or Spring; third-year and later students may register for up to 22 credit hours.

### UET - Minimum Attendance Requirement and Short Attendance Consequence

A minimum of 75% attendance is required in each subject. If a student's attendance falls below that threshold, the student receives a WF grade.

> Source note: This confirms the CE-29 session figure (75%) and adds the consequence of short attendance, which is a WF grade.

### UET Undergraduate - Grading Rules

Under UET's Undergraduate Semester Regulations the subject teacher awards letter grades after semester-long assessment, in consultation with the department chairperson. Type-A subjects are graded on a relative scale. Type-B and Type-C subjects may use an absolute scale if the teacher considers it appropriate. A student below the established minimum content-mastery threshold receives an F. GPA and CGPA are calculated under the regulations' prescribed grade-point formula. Students may repeat courses to improve grades, subject to the maximum credit-hour registration limit.

### UET Undergraduate - Result Declaration Timeline

Grades first appear as provisional on the Examination Portal; official results are declared after scrutiny and verification, normally within one week after all results are received.

### UET Undergraduate - Examination Rules

The Undergraduate Semester Regulations govern both teaching and examinations. Results are submitted by teachers to the Controller of Examinations; after the scheduled final-examination period the Controller reports result status to the Vice Chancellor within six working days. Examination records are retained for future reference. A student may request a grade change through Form Sem-1 at the Chairperson's Office, stating the specific reason.

Keywords: examination rules, exam rules, grade change, form sem-1, recheck, revaluation, controller of examinations, result grievance, exam policy, how results are processed

### UET Undergraduate - Examination and Summer Registration Fees

The undergraduate examination fee is included in each semester's recurring charges: Rs. 1,474 (subsidized) or Rs. 1,572 (partially subsidized). Summer-semester registration carries an additional per-credit-hour registration fee; the current undergraduate schedule lists Rs. 2,650 as the summer subject-registration fee.

### UET Fall 2026 - Admission Application Fee and ECAT Dates

The undergraduate admission application fee is Rs. 3,000, and one application can cover multiple program choices. For Fall 2026, online undergraduate applications opened on June 8, 2026 and closed on July 15, 2026. ECAT remains mandatory for Computer Engineering, and the test includes combinations such as English, Physics, Mathematics and Computer Science.

> Source note: These dates belong to the Fall 2026 admission cycle and are not current or upcoming dates.
