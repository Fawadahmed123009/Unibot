### Department of Computer Engineering - Degree Programs Offered - Fall 2026

Computer Engineering is offered by UET Lahore's Department of Computer Engineering. The department offers the following degree programs:

- B.Sc. Computer Engineering
- B.Sc. Cyber Security
- B.Sc. Information Systems and Technology
- M.Sc. Computer Engineering
- Ph.D. Computer Engineering

### Computer Engineering - Minimum Eligibility for Admission

For admission to Computer Engineering a student needs at least 60% marks in Intermediate or an equivalent qualification, and must take the UET ECAT. The ECAT is compulsory for Engineering, Computing, Architecture and related disciplines.
