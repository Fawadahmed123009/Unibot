### Computer Engineering - Minimum Eligibility for Admission

For admission to Computer Engineering a student needs at least 60% marks in Intermediate or an equivalent qualification, and must take the UET ECAT. The ECAT is compulsory for Engineering, Computing, Architecture and related disciplines.
